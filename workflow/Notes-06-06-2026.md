The border of Golem Battle Img is the gradient of the Dom and Pas elements as in the Golem Builder. The elemental icons are also present in the Golem Battle Img, but smaller and in the corners. The Item, Enchant, and Curse slots are represented by small icons in square frames that can be filled with the corresponding content when equipped.

## Computer Opponents
Computer Opponents will have preference variables that determine its playstyle as well as its setup golem team. That way, it is dynamic and can be reused across multiple matches, and can also be used for testing specific scenarios.
> For example, a Computer Opponent with a high "Aggression" preference will prioritize equipping offensive Items and playing ATK-boosting Enchantments, while one with a high "Defense" preference will focus on defensive Items and DEF-boosting Enchantments.
